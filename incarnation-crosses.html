Incarnation Crosses

Your Incarnation Cross is the single theme behind your life process, born from the angle between your conscious and unconscious Sun/Earth gates.

Reference: Jovian Archive — Incarnation Crosses

A life theme, not a destination

The Incarnation Cross is often confused with purpose. In Human Design, it is more accurate to see it as a consistent life process. You live the cross by aligning with Strategy and Authority, not by achieving outcomes.

Right vs left angle

Right-angle crosses generally operate through relationships and lived example. Left-angle crosses operate through interior design and cumulative contribution. Both are valid; both require correct living.
