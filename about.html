The Reflector's Journey

From the Chaos of the Ground to the Clarity of the Roof.

For thirty years, I moved through periods of intense trial and error. I absorbed everything around me like a sponge, never quite sure what was really mine. It landed as a heavy, quiet disappointment.

At 35, something shifted. The frantic experimentation slowed. Now I sit in this space of watching and waiting, letting life process itself through me rather than pushing for answers.

I built The Cosmic Mirror because I know what it feels like to carry the wrong things. My work is patient observation—holding up a mirror so you can set down the weight of living someone else's idea of you.
