Profiles

Your Profile is two Lines joined together: one conscious, one unconscious. It describes the roles you move through as you mature.

Reference: Jovian Archive — The 6 Lines

How profiles work

The first Line is often unconscious, the second Line often conscious—or vice versa depending on your specific profile. Together they create a lifelong study of behavior and purpose through interaction.

The 6/2 roof

A popular example is the 6/2, where the 6th Line eventually becomes the role-modeling stage of life, with the 2nd Line underneath bringing innate talent and withdrawal cycles. Profiles are not personality tests; they are behavioral roles.
