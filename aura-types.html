Aura Types

Each Human Design Type projects a recognizable aura pattern. These patterns shape how others perceive you before you even speak.

Reference: Jovian Archive — What Is Human Design

Overview

Aura is not a mood. In Human Design, aura is the consistent electromagnetic environment your Type creates around you. It determines how others approach you, what they expect from you, and where resistance often shows up.

The Four Types

Manifestors, Generators, Manifesting Generators, Projectors, and Reflectors each carry a distinct aura. Each aura is mechanically valid. Correct aura behavior reduces friction and increases recognition.

Recognition and resistance

When you express your aura correctly, others recognize you. When you mask or override it, invitations turn into demands, and availability turns into burnout.
