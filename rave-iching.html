Rave I'Ching

The 64 Gates mapped onto the I'Ching, with the 6 Lines of each hexagram.

Overview

In Human Design, each Gate corresponds to an I'Ching hexagram. Each hexagram contains 6 Lines, and those Lines add the behavioral layers within the Gate.

Reading the Lines

1st Line — Investigator: Introspective, grounded, detail-oriented. Needs time to build a secure foundation.

2nd Line — Hermit: Natural talent that needs seclusion to develop and express without performance.

3rd Line — Martyr: Trial and error. Learns by doing, often through mistakes and adjustment.

4th Line — Opportunist: Network and extension. Needs invitation or recognition to share gifts.

5th Line — Heretic: Universal worker. Projected onto as a savior or problem-solver; authority and trust matter.

6th Line — Role Model: A 3-phase arc — trial, observation, role-model presence. Usually realized later in life.
