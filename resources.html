01 — The Five Auras

Aura Types

Your aura is your electromagnetic field — the boundary between you and the universe. Each Type has a unique aura that determines how you interact with life, make decisions, and fulfill your purpose.

✦ Featured — The Rarest

The Reflector

The rarest type — approximately 1% of the population. Reflectors have no Definition in their chart. Every single center is open, making them a pure, undistorted mirror of whatever environment and people surround them. They do not generate their own consistent energy — they sample the wholeness of others.

Strategy

Wait a Lunar Cycle — 28 days of processing before major decisions

Authority

Lunar — clarity unfolds through the moon's phases over time

Signature (Aligned)

Surprise — wonder, awe, the transcendent feeling of right environment

Not-Self (Misaligned)

Disappointment — the bitter signal of wrong people or places

A Reflector's open design is not a vulnerability — it is their superpower. They are here to reflect the health of their community, to be the mirror that shows others what is truly happening.

Non-Sacral · Impacting

Manifestor

The only type designed to initiate action independently. Their aura is closed and repelling — they impact the world simply by existing.

  • ◆ Strategy: Inform before initiating
  • ◆ Signature: Peace
  • ◆ Not-Self: Anger
  • ◆ Motor: Heart → Throat
Sacral · ~70% of Population

Generator

The builders and life-force of the world. Their defined sacral center generates sustainable energy. They are here to respond, not initiate.

  • ◆ Strategy: Respond to life
  • ◆ Authority: Sacral (uh-huh / unh-unh)
  • ◆ Signature: Satisfaction
  • ◆ Not-Self: Frustration
Non-Sacral · ~20% of Population

Projector

The guides and directors of the world. Their focused, absorbing aura penetrates the sacral of others. They are here to manage and guide Generator energy — but only when recognized and invited.

  • ◆ Strategy: Wait for the invitation
  • ◆ Signature: Success
  • ◆ Not-Self: Bitterness
  • ◆ Gift: Reading others' energy
Sacral · Hybrid

Manifesting Generator

A hybrid of Manifestor speed and Generator power. They can initiate AND respond. Their energy moves in spiraling, efficient bursts — multi-passionate and designed to skip steps.

  • ◆ Strategy: Respond, then inform
  • ◆ Authority: Sacral / Emotional
  • ◆ Signature: Satisfaction + Peace
  • ◆ Not-Self: Frustration + Anger

02 — The Twelve Roles

Profiles

Your Profile is the costume you wear in life — formed by the conscious and unconscious lines of your Sun/Earth. The first line is your personality (conscious); the second is your design (unconscious).

✦ Featured — 6/2 Profile

The Role Model / Hermit

The 6th line is the transpersonal — a being who moves through three distinct life phases. The 2nd line is the Hermit — a natural genius coded for solitude. Together: the Role Model who withdraws to perfect their gifts, then emerges as a beacon for others.

Phase 1: 0–30 (Martyr)

The 3rd line unconsciously bumps into life. Through trial, error, and broken bonds, foundational wisdom is forged.

Phase 2: 30–50 (Hermit)

The door closes. Deep inner work in solitude. The 2nd line's gifts are perfected away from the noise of the world.

Phase 3: 50+ (Role Model)

Emergence. The wisdom gathered in withdrawal becomes something others can trust, follow, and learn from.

1/3 — Investigator / Martyr

The foundational researcher. Through trial and error (3rd line), they discover what is stable and true (1st line).

1/4 — Investigator / Opportunist

Deep focus (1st line) combined with a tight, powerful network (4th line). Opportunity flows through others.

2/4 — Hermit / Opportunist

A hermit with natural charisma. Their network is their net worth — but only when they honor their need for solitude.

2/5 — Hermit / Heretic

A hermit whose gifts are projected onto by others (5th line). They must retreat (2nd line) to discover what is truly theirs.

3/5 — Martyr / Heretic

The universalizer. Through trial (3rd line), they find practical solutions that work for everyone (5th line).

3/6 — Martyr / Role Model

A lifelong trial-and-error process (3rd line) that transitions into becoming a transpersonal authority (6th line).

4/6 — Opportunist / Role Model

The over-achiever. Their close relationships (4th line) become the foundation for transpersonal wisdom (6th line).

4/1 — Opportunist / Investigator

Fixed focus through deep study (1st line) combined with a powerful network (4th line).

5/1 — Heretic / Investigator

The practical visionary. Deep investigation (1st line) produces solutions that seem almost magical (5th line).

5/2 — Heretic / Hermit

A hermit whose gifts are projected onto by others (5th line). They must retreat (2nd line) to discover their truth.

6/3 — Role Model / Martyr

A role model who learns through constant trial and error (3rd line). Their transpersonal impact (6th line) is forged in fire.

03 — The Nine Hubs

Energy Centers

The nine centers are the bioenergetic hubs of your bodygraph. Defined centers (colored in) operate consistently — they are your reliable strengths. Undefined centers (white) are where you absorb, amplify, and learn from the world.

Head Center (Inspiration)

Biological: Brain, pineal gland, visual cortex

Defined: Consistent inspiration, fixed ways of thinking, mental pressure to engage

Undefined: Open to all kinds of inspiration, absorbing others' questions, no fixed mental pressure

Ajna Center (Processing)

Biological: Pituitary gland, left/right brain hemispheres

Defined: Fixed way of processing information, consistent analysis, sureness in how they think

Undefined: Flexible thinking, absorbing others' mental frameworks, learns by listening

Throat Center (Communication)

Biological: Thyroid, parathyroid, vocal cords, metabolism

Defined: Consistent communication style, reliable voice, metabolic consistency

Undefined: Adapts speech to environment, may speak to fill pressure, learns others' communication styles

G Center (Identity / Love)

Biological: Liver, blood serum, immune system

Defined: Fixed sense of self, consistent direction in life, reliable love nature

Undefined: Fluid identity, love adapts to whom they're with, direction comes from environment

Heart Center (Ego / Will)

Biological: Heart, stomach, gall bladder, T-cells

Defined: Consistent willpower, reliable ego, strong sense of material commitment and contracts

Undefined: Amplifies others' will, no fixed ego pattern, learns through others' ambition

Sacral Center (Life Force)

Biological: Ovaries, uterus, kidneys, reproductive system

Defined: Sustainable life-force energy, reliable reproduction/creativity, generational power

Undefined: No consistent energy source, borrows others' sacral power, must rest intentionally

Solar Plexus (Emotions)

Biological: Pancreas, lungs, nervous system, prostate

Defined: Emotional wave — must wait out the wave for clarity; peak to trough before deciding

Undefined: Avoids confrontation, absorbs and amplifies others' emotions, no emotional wave of own

Splenic Center (Intuition)

Biological: Spleen, lymphatic system, immune function, T-cells

Defined: Reliable intuition, consistent immune system, body wisdom that can be trusted moment-to-moment

Undefined: Adapts to environment's safety, may hold onto things/people for comfort, learns others' fears

Root Center (Pressure)

Biological: Adrenal glands, kidneys, prostate, basal ganglia

Defined: Consistent pressure to move, reliable stress response, adrenaline flows in a fixed pattern

Undefined: Amplifies others' stress, no internal pressure source, must learn to release borrowed adrenaline
