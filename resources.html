Start Here

A first orientation, not a textbook.

Human Design is most useful when you enter through your own curiosity. If you are new, begin with your Aura Type or your Profile. From there, use the encyclopedia like a map: follow one gate, one channel, or one center until something clicks.

How to use this
  • 1. Pick one category that feels personal.
  • 2. Read the entry in full, not just the summary.
  • 3. Notice where it matches your experience.
  • 4. Bring one question into your evaluation.

This encyclopedia is a mirror, not a rulebook. Let it reflect back what you already recognize.
