Channels

A Channel forms only when both Gates on either side of a connection are defined in your chart. It creates a consistent, reliable current between Centers.

Reference: Jovian Archive — Gates & Hexagrams

Overview

Channels are the wiring of the BodyGraph. They connect Centers and create a reliable stream of energy from one Center to another. While Gates are entry points, Channels are the bridges between them.

How to read a channel

Start from the Gates on each side, note the Centers involved, and read the relationship as a sustained theme—not a passing impulse.

Living it

Channels show where you naturally have reliable energy, but conditioning can distort them into pressure or compulsion. Strategy and Authority keep the channel honest.
